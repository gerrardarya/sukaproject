# Impeccable Design Context — Custom at Suka

## Design Context

### Users

**Dual audience, equal weight:**

1. **Corporate buyers** — HR managers, executive assistants, event planners. Ordering in quantity for client gifting, employee milestones, and company events. Primarily desktop, weekday working hours. Need to feel the brand is reliable, established, and worth presenting to their stakeholders.

2. **Personal gifters** — Individuals planning weddings, baby showers, birthdays, anniversaries. Often discovered via Instagram or word of mouth. Mobile-first, emotionally invested. Need to feel the gift will be special and personal, not generic.

Both audiences arrive wanting to be *impressed before they inquire*. The interface is the first gift they receive.

### Brand Personality

**Warm. Luxurious. Trustworthy. Joyful.**

All four — simultaneously and without contradiction. The brand is a premium Indonesian gift house: it feels like a boutique you'd step into on a quiet afternoon and immediately know you're in the right place. Not sterile luxury. Warm luxury. The kind of warmth that comes from genuine craft and attention, not from loud cheerfulness.

Three-word distillation: **Refined. Handcrafted. Celebratory.**

### Aesthetic Direction

**Reference:** Soft luxury — Aesop, Cos. Quiet confidence. Restrained visual language. Warmth communicated through material and texture, not color saturation or busyness.

**Palette:** Cream (`#f8f7f4`) and warm gold (`#c9a961`) are the brand soul — keep and deepen them. Tint all neutrals toward the gold hue. Gold appears sparingly, like a wax seal: ceremonial, never decorative filler.

**Typography:** Poppins is currently used everywhere — explore a more editorial pairing. A refined, distinctive serif or high-contrast display face for headings paired with a clean humanist for body. Nothing in the reflex-font reject list.

**Theme:** Light. This brand is browsed during planning moments — a quiet Sunday, a lunch break, an afternoon at the office. The product photos are the star; the interface is the stage.

**Anti-references:** Avoid anything that reads as generic e-commerce (rounded cards with drop shadows, loud CTAs, busy grids). No cyan-on-dark tech aesthetic. Nothing that could be any brand — everything should feel distinctly Custom at Suka.

### Design Principles

1. **Warmth through restraint** — Luxury is communicated by what is *not* there. No clutter, no cheap decoration, generous whitespace, unhurried hierarchy. Let the product photography breathe.

2. **Gold as ceremony** — The accent color is used at inflection points only: a key label, a hover reveal, a section marker. Never background fill, never gradient, never decoration for its own sake.

3. **Dual confidence** — A corporate buyer and a personal gifter arrive at the same page. Both should feel this is exactly the right place — one sees reliability and polish, the other sees heart and beauty. The interface should carry both readings without strain.

4. **Indonesian premium identity** — Not a generic luxury template. Grounded in artisan craft, celebratory occasions, and the warmth of Indonesian gift culture. Design choices should feel like they belong to this specific brand, not to "luxury e-commerce" as a category.

5. **Whitespace as hospitality** — Every section breathes. Layouts never rush the eye. Generous padding above headings, tight groupings for related elements, then open air before the next idea.
